# Datasets and Benchmarks

The assignment requires at least three relevant datasets/benchmarks, with name, source, description, application, and link.

## 1. HaluEval
- **Source:** RUCAIBox / EMNLP 2023
- **Description:** Large-scale hallucination evaluation benchmark covering knowledge-based dialogue, summarization, and knowledge-based QA.
- **Application:** Evaluate hallucination recognition and compare detection methods.
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.397/
- **Data / code:** https://github.com/RUCAIBox/HaluEval

## 2. RAGTruth
- **Source:** ACL 2024 / ParticleMedia
- **Description:** Nearly 18,000 RAG responses with case-level and word-level hallucination annotations.
- **Application:** Fine-grained detection and mitigation of unsupported or contradictory RAG claims.
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.585/
- **Data / code:** https://github.com/ParticleMedia/RAGTruth

## 3. TruthfulQA
- **Source:** ACL 2022 / official project repository
- **Description:** 817 questions across 38 categories designed to test whether models reproduce common human falsehoods.
- **Application:** Evaluate truthfulness and reliability of model answers.
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.229/
- **Data / code:** https://github.com/sylinrl/truthfulqa

## 4. ALCE
- **Source:** EMNLP 2023 / Princeton NLP
- **Description:** Benchmark for evaluating LLM-generated answers with citations; includes ASQA, QAMPARI, and ELI5.
- **Application:** Evaluate citation correctness, completeness, and fluency.
- **Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/
- **Data / code:** https://github.com/princeton-nlp/ALCE

## 5. FELM
- **Source:** NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks
- **Description:** Fine-grained factuality benchmark with segment-level annotations across five domains.
- **Application:** Evaluate factuality evaluators and inspect specific factual errors.
- **Paper:** https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/8b8a7960d343e023a6a0afe37eee6022-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf
- **Data / code:** https://github.com/hkust-nlp/felm
